# Artistic Diagnostic Workflow

Use when an image fails its brief and the cause is uncertain. Read the [interpretation boundaries](painting-to-cg-boundaries.md) before converting painting advice into physical settings.

| Symptom | Competing explanations | Small useful test | Recheck |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building loses attention | Foreground value, hue, sharpness or texture competes | Reduce one competitor in a temporary comparison before brightening the building | First read in full frame and at delivery size |
| Facade looks flat | Merged planes, weak overlap, missing relief, illumination or scale cues | Inspect returns, soffits and openings without adding fog | Architecture remains accurate and depth is legible |
| Material looks wrong | Scale, relief, environment, reflection or exposure | Hold material fixed and compare broad versus grazing light or a reflection card | Intended camera, not only close-up test |
| Shadow color looks wrong | Sky, ground bounce, material color, other sources or grading | Temporarily neutralize a suspected contributor with other variables fixed | Restore intended materials; retain plausible color |
| Mood feels wrong | Viewer role, activity, light and framing disagree | Follow [Mood Design](mood-design.md) and isolate an inconsistent cue | Situation and required architectural information |
| Scene seems miniature | Inconsistent doors, windows, floor heights or camera relationships | Compare a few familiar scale anchors before adding detail | Physical dimensions and projected proportions |

Treat the result as evidence for a hypothesis, not proof that every other parameter is correct. Restore diagnostic substitutions unless deliberately adopted within the brief. A paintover is a visual test, not proof of a feasible scene change.

Use the [illustrated case studies](book-case-studies.md) for concrete sources. For final evaluation use the [artistic review checklist](artistic-review-checklist.md). Stop when the requested problem is resolved and required information remains clear; do not optimize every contrast or detail indiscriminately.
