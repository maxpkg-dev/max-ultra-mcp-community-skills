# Interior
1 Define room purpose and emotional read.
2 Choose humanly meaningful camera height/viewpoint.
3 Establish primary focal point.
4 Use furniture, walls, ceiling/floor seams and light as compositional structures.
5 Control wide-angle distortion; do not go wider merely to show everything.
6 Separate spatial planes with overlap/value/light/detail.
7 Establish daylight/artificial-light relationship.
8 Prevent windows, fixtures, artwork, screens and highlights from stealing hierarchy.
9 Validate material scale/reflection behavior.
10 Style props selectively.
11 Thumbnail + grayscale tests.
12 Technical pass.
