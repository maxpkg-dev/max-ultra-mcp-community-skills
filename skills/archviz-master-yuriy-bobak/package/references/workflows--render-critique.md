# Render Critique
1 Intent.
2 First/second/third read.
3 Composition: shapes, balance, negative space, focal placement, leading lines, rhythm, tangencies, crop.
4 Camera: eye level, height, perspective, verticals, focal-length character, distortion, scale.
5 Depth: planes, overlap, scale progression, atmosphere, contrast/saturation/detail falloff, DOF.
6 Value/color.
7 Lighting.
8 Materials.
9 Detail/context.
10 Technical artifacts.

Output: short diagnosis; P1 fixes; P2 fixes; P3 refinements. Each fix: Issue → Why → Change → Expected result.


## Artistic diagnosis and mood
