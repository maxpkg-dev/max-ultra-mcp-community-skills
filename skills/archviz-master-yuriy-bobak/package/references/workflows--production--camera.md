# Camera — Production Workflow

## Load
composition, perspective-camera, depth, visual-hierarchy; 3ds Max core; Corona camera/current baseline.

## Decision tree
What must the shot communicate?
→ human experience: natural eye-level starting point.
→ monumentality: lower POV may be intentional.
→ site/context: elevated/bird's-eye candidate.
→ frontal facade/order: one-point/near-frontal candidate.
→ volume/corner relationship: two-point candidate.

Then:
1. camera-to-subject distance;
2. focal-length character/FOV;
3. vertical behavior;
4. foreground exaggeration;
5. scale cues;
6. crop/negative space;
7. leading lines;
8. depth landmarks.

## Compare alternatives
Judge each camera by architectural information, emotional character, distortion, depth and hierarchy—not by how much geometry fits.

## Max Ultra execution
Use [Max Ultra MCP Integration](max-ultra-integration.md) and the available camera-composition skill for live edits. Record output aspect, activate the camera, deterministically enable Safe Frame and inspect the final render area in every comparison. Apply this workflow's artistic framing ahead of generic centering defaults within the brief; preserve explicit full-object or locked-camera requirements. An intentional crop needs separate overview evidence if complete geometry must also be verified.
