# Narrative & Art Direction
Narrative in ArchViz can be a clear proposition about experiencing architecture rather than a literal story.

Possible intents: arrival, calm residential life, public activity, luxury, nature integration, monumentality, intimacy, craftsmanship, urban energy, hospitality, seasonal/weather character.

Build narrative through viewpoint, focal point, people/actions, vehicles, time of day, weather, landscape, visible interior activity, props and visual movement.

Entourage should support the architectural story, not merely fill space.


## Expanded book-grounded diagnosis

Source keys L, C and P and page-number conventions are defined in the [evidence register](book-evidence-register.md). ArchViz applications are production interpretations, not software instructions from the books.

### Narrative depends on relationships and viewpoint

C 197-219 examines implication, gaze and viewpoint; C 231 explores alternative placements. The same figure can suggest arrival or departure through orientation and position.

**ArchViz application:** place people and activity to support a specific use of the space. A person facing an entrance, a recently used seat or a lit room may communicate more than extra decorative assets. Respect the brief and avoid suggesting uses the design does not support.
