## Pipeline
1. Intent — identify hero feature, target audience, mood and architectural message.
2. Camera — establish eye level, camera distance, perspective character, crop and vertical behavior.
3. Blockout — judge architecture/context as large shapes before detailing.
4. Depth — design foreground, subject, background and far background using overlap, scale and atmospheric hierarchy.
5. Procedural context — RailClone for repeated architectural/site systems; ForestPack for vegetation and dense environment assets.
6. Lighting — choose daylight/HDRI/artificial strategy that describes form and supports hierarchy.
7. Materials — establish physical identity, scale, roughness/reflection/Fresnel before imperfections.
8. Vegetation/entourage — add only where it supports framing, scale and story.
9. IR lookdev — iterate broad light/value/material decisions.
10. Final Corona production render — choose render limit according to production needs; denoise only after adequate convergence.
11. VFB/post — finish, do not repair unresolved scene problems.
12. QA — thumbnail, grayscale, tangencies, repetition, mapping, floating assets, noise, fireflies and client brief.

## Stop conditions
Do not proceed to high-detail vegetation/material imperfections if camera and large-value hierarchy are unresolved.
